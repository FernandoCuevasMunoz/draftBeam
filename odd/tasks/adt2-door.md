# Puerta ADT2 (YQArch) para draftBeam
Feature: rediseño completo de la puerta 2D con flujo de dos toques sobre el muro.
Route: inline ODD on `feature/doors-windows`; update this file and its Engram mirror with every task transition.

## Specs
1. S1. User: "Está mal diseñada la puerta, quiero que elimines el rastro de la ejecupción de esta." The previous door design is removed from the code: four-measurement dialog (`DoorDimensions`), draggable ghost block with insertion marker, start/center/end offset snapping, placement panel, and frame+leaf geometry whose hole is `2 × frameWidth + leafWidth`. Windows must keep working.
2. S2. User: "Quiero diseñarla como tranaja el plugin de AutoCad YQARCH, con su ejecutable ADT2." Quoted behaviour: "ADT2 calcula automáticamente el espacio entre dos clics, abre el vano en el muro (hace el corte) e inserta el bloque de la puerta alineado perfectamente al grosor del tabique." Two taps over a wall define the opening, the wall is cut there, and the door aligns to the wall thickness.
3. S3. User: "Primero se debe seleccionar el ancho de la puerta, que sería el ancho del vano, (se calcula en su parte media puede ser del muro)". The opening width is the distance between the two taps projected on the wall mid-line (axis), and it equals the door leaf width.
4. S4. User: "después selecciono el ancho del muro y al darle enter me aparece la puerta con su marco empotrado entre ambos muros". Frame depth equals the wall thickness and the frame is embedded between both wall faces; confirming inserts the door; no measurement dialog is shown before placing it.
5. S5. User: "el usuario define el angulo que mejor defina la puerta". The opening angle is chosen by the user, not fixed at 90°; the same gesture chooses the opening side. The hinge sits on the first tap. Hinge, angle and side stay editable from the wall card.
6. S6. Session decisions: three-step confirmation by a third gesture that chooses the opening side; new commits delete and rebuild the door (no history rewrite, no force push); plans holding the previous door format are dropped with a notice instead of being converted.
7. S7. Approved design rules: door placement requires a wall with width greater than zero, an opening of at least 100 mm, an opening that fits inside the wall, and no overlap with other openings.
8. S8. Persistence: the private draft and the DXF keep the new door model (offset, opening width, hinge, angle, side, leaf thickness); importing the previous door format drops those doors with a count notice while keeping walls and windows.
13. S9. User: "Hay un problema, pincho donde termina el muro y luego el otro extremo pero no lo toma al parecer. es posible que se resalte la linea donde pincho para cver si la tommó como muro?" While placing a door the chosen wall must stay highlighted, the first anchor must stay marked on the wall mid-line, and the measured opening must be drawn, so a rejected tap is never silent.
9. S10. User: "Lo que yo necesito es que se inserte la puerta pero en un vano ya existente en el plano que yo dejo." Example given: two horizontal walls of 1000 mm with 100 mm width, an 800 mm gap between them, and the result must be "muro de 1000 mm, puerta con marco, y muro de 1000 mm. No quiero que la puerta se inserte en un muro existente." The door is therefore a stand-alone plan entity, never a wall opening.
10. S11. User: "al pincha el extremo del muro 1 y luego el extremo del muro 2, con esto se indica el ancho del vano". Both taps snap to wall ends; the gap between those two vertices is the opening.
11. S12. User: "después que me pida el ancho del muro. Por lo que pincharé en eje Y el primer vertice y Y- en segundo vertice". After the gap, a depth step asks for the wall thickness measured with two taps across the gap; the width of the neighbouring walls is proposed when both agree.
12. S13. User: "luego viene hacia donde quiero que abra la puerta". After the depth, the pointer sets the opening side and angle and a tap confirms. Session decisions: frame width is asked once before measuring (default 50 mm) and the leaf is `gap − 2 × frame`; walls are never cut or modified.
14. S14. User: "Quiero que quede por defecto que la profundidad del marco sea del mismo ancho del marco" clarified as: "Tiene que ser la profundidad del muro. Si ponemos el ancho del marco en 50 mm, y el ancho del muro es de 100 mm. Entonces, en eje horizontal supongamos, 1000mm (muro 1), 50 (ancho de marco) 700 puerta, 50 ancho del marco y 1000 muro 2, En eje Y el marco debe quedar de 100 en este caso, ya que el muro es de 100" and "Se ajusta la profundidad del marco al ancho del muro y queda de manera continua". The frame depth is the wall thickness, taken automatically, with no depth taps.
15. S15. User: "Al cambiar el ángulo que sea 0 90 180 270 360°". The leaf angle is chosen in 90° steps: 0 and 360 closed over the gap, 90 and 270 perpendicular on either side, 180 back over the gap. The swing sign disappears; the angle alone declares side and direction.
16. S16. User: "ahora, esta puerta debe estar dentro de una capa. Puede ser capa de puerta" and chose one single layer: every door is exported to `FDXL_PUERTA`.
